Privacy policy

Finances for iPhone · Last updated 16 October 2026

Who uses the app

Finances is a personal budgeting app. It is built and used by one person, its developer, for their own accounts only. It is not distributed to the public, and nobody else's data is processed. The developer is both the only user and the person responsible for the data.

What data is read

  • Balances and transactions of the developer's own payment accounts, read through Enable Banking Oy, an account information service provider licensed by the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority.
  • Access is read-only. The app never initiates payments or transfers.

Where the data is kept

  • Only on the developer's iPhone, in the app's storage protected by iOS Data Protection and locked behind Face ID.
  • Optionally, an encrypted backup (AES-GCM, password-derived key) in the developer's own iCloud Drive.
  • There is no server, no database outside the phone, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Access keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on the device only, never in the app's code or in any repository.

Who else receives data

  • Enable Banking Oy and the account-holding banks, to retrieve the data the developer authorised.
  • Optional AI features, used only when the developer explicitly asks: the figures or transaction labels needed for that request are sent to the AI provider the developer chose (Anthropic or Google), with their own API key.
  • Data is never sold, shared for marketing or used for any other purpose.

How long it is kept

Data stays on the phone until the developer deletes it in the app. Bank access ends when the consent given at the bank expires (at most 180 days) and can be revoked at any time in the app, at Enable Banking or at the bank.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.
